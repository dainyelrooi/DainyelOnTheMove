The Road to Machu Picchu Begins! 🏔️

By Dainyel · Sent 11 November 2026

POSTCARDS FROM DAINYEL Take the scenic route.

The next adventure is about to begin! 🇵🇪

On Today November 11 , I'm taking on the legendary 2-day Short Inca Trail to Machu Picchu ! 🥾 Around 12 km of ancient stone paths, mountain scenery and Inca ruins, with the incredible Sun Gate along the way.

Did you know? The Inca Trail was part of an enormous road network connecting the Inca Empire. And now I get to walk a little piece of that history myself!

Of course, I'll be filming the entire adventure , so expect some incredible new videos, behind-the-scenes moments and probably a few exhausted reactions from me. 😂🎥

While I'm out exploring, you can catch up on my previous travel videos on my YouTube channel .

Wish me luck, and stay tuned. The adventure is only getting started! 🌎

— Dainyel
