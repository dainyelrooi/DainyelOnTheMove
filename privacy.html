Privacy, simply.

What happens when you use an interactive feature on this website.

Last updated: 14 October 2026. This is a working notice for the current features of DainyelOnTheMove. Details will be reviewed as the website develops.

Questions and community conversations

Personal questions store your selected topic, question, publication consent and optional nickname. Community questions and replies store a nickname, topic or conversation, message and publication consent. No email address is requested for Q&A. Submissions remain private until Dainyel approves them. Approved questions, answers and replies can be read publicly.

Follow the trip / Field Notes

If you choose to sign up, the site stores your email, consent, subscription status and time of registration in a private list. No automatic confirmation or email delivery provider is configured yet. Registration saves your interest in future updates. You can ask Dainyel to remove your details through Instagram ↗.

Contacting Dainyel

The collaboration form prepares text on your device. It does not send that text to this website or store it in the website database. You choose whether to copy and send it through Instagram, which has its own privacy policy.

Firebase and security

The site uses Google Firebase for owner sign-in, database services and protection against abuse. Security checks may process technical data such as an IP address and device/browser information when you use protected forms. Only the authorised owner can manage private submissions and the subscriber list. Records remain until the owner removes them; an automatic deletion schedule has not been configured.

Videos and external websites

Videos hosted with this site do not require loading YouTube or Instagram. An external embedded player loads only after you press its load button. At that point the platform can receive information such as your IP address and use cookies or similar technologies under its own policy. See the cookie notice.

Location updates

A manually shared check-in contains only a city and country. The website does not publish an exact device location.

Questions or removal requests

Contact DainyelOnTheMove on Instagram ↗ about your information, a published contribution or a request to leave the list. Please identify the contribution or signup you are asking about.
