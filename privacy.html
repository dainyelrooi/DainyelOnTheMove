Your information

Privacy, simply.

What I collect, why I need it and how you can stay in control.

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Who is responsible?

Dainyel, the Netherlands, is responsible for personal information collected through DainyelOnTheMove. For privacy questions, withdrawal of consent or a request about your information, email support@dainyelonthemove.com. Include only enough information to identify your contribution or sign-up.

Questions and community conversations

If you submit a question, I store the topic, message, time, publication consent and optional nickname. Community questions and replies also store their conversation reference and moderation status. Q&A does not ask for your email address. Only the owner can review private submissions. Approved contributions and my answers are public, so other people and search engines may read them.

To help me review new questions, the question text, topic and any nickname are included in private email notifications delivered through Brevo to Dainyel. This does not subscribe you to Postcards. Please leave out private contact details or sensitive information.

The legal basis for receiving and publishing your contribution is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Submitting is optional. You can withdraw publication consent through the contact channel above; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Postcards from Dainyel

The website includes an email sign-up form connected to Brevo. It asks for your email address and agreement to receive weekly destination facts and occasional travel stories, films and updates. Brevo processes your address, consent and subscription records to manage and deliver these emails for Dainyel. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Your address is not published or sold.

After you submit the form with your consent and complete the CAPTCHA, you join the Postcards list immediately. A welcome email follows; no email confirmation click is required. Messages come from postcards@dainyelonthemove.com. You can unsubscribe using the link in the emails or contact support@dainyelonthemove.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

The hosted form uses Google reCAPTCHA to prevent abuse. Brevo and Google may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser details when the form loads and you use it; email delivery and links may also generate technical activity records. Read Brevo’s privacy policy ↗ and Google’s privacy policy ↗ for their processing and international transfer information. The form and its CAPTCHA load when you open the sign-up form. A direct Brevo form link is also available if the embedded form is unavailable.

Earlier Field Notes interest registrations remain private in Firebase. They have not been imported into Brevo or treated as confirmed subscribers. You can ask for removal through the contact address above.

Community challenges

Challenge suggestions and optional nicknames remain private until the owner approves them. Edits return to review. Keep the private edit key to change your own suggestion; the key is not included in public content. Necessary abuse protection uses Firebase App Check and short-lived, hashed rate-limit identifiers. No visitor GPS is collected.

How long information is kept

  • Unpublished questions and replies: up to 90 days after submission for review. Rejected submissions are removed when no longer needed for moderation.
  • Published contributions: while the contribution remains part of a relevant public conversation, or until you withdraw consent or request removal. These are reviewed at least annually; there is no fixed expiry for the public story archive.
  • Newsletter and earlier Field Notes registrations: up to 24 months after registration, or sooner if you withdraw or the updates service ends. After unsubscribing, only the minimum record needed to honour your opt-out is retained; it is not used to send updates.
  • Privacy requests: only for resolving the request and documenting its outcome, for up to 12 months after closure, unless a specific legal obligation or dispute requires longer retention.

Website records are reviewed and removed manually by the owner; there is no automatic deletion job. Google controls the retention of its service security logs separately. For example, Firebase Hosting retains request IP data for a few months, as described in Firebase’s privacy information ↗.

Website hosting, database and security

Google Firebase provides hosting, Firestore, owner authentication and abuse protection. Network and browser details, including IP addresses and security tokens, may be processed to deliver the site, prevent spam and protect access. I rely on the legitimate interest of operating a secure, reliable website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for necessary security processing. You can object through the contact channel above.

Only the authorised owner can manage private submissions and registrations. Google processes service data under its applicable terms. Some Google services process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States; the relevant safeguards are described in Google’s data processing terms ↗. Optional Google Analytics is described below. Advertising is off in the current Analytics-only phase.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures visits and page usage to help improve this website. The current website privacy message offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options for Analytics only. The Analytics tag stays unloaded until you accept. Advertising and its consent service are off for now, so Analytics does not depend on AdSense approval. Rejecting does not prevent reading stories or signing up. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Google processes visit information, page URLs, browser/device details and cookie identifiers under its applicable terms, potentially outside the EEA. Newsletter addresses, question text and account details are not sent as custom analytics events. Configured page locations exclude query strings and arbitrary fragments. Known public panels such as the atlas, Q&A and films use fixed names so their visits can be counted separately. Search text and unknown error-page paths are not sent. Google signals and advertising personalisation features within Google Analytics remain disabled.

Your Analytics-only choice is saved in this browser for up to 180 days. An expired, invalid or missing record asks again. Change or withdraw your choice on the Cookies & settings page. Withdrawing stops subsequent measurement, clears this site's Analytics cookies and reloads the page; it does not remove records already sent. Analytics cookies have a configured lifetime of up to six months. Contact support@dainyelonthemove.com about earlier data. The Analytics account is configured to retain event-level data for two months and user-level data for fourteen months. The user-level retention period resets on new activity. These controls do not determine the retention of most aggregated standard reports. Read Google's privacy policy.

Advertising is off for now

The website retains the public AdSense ownership metadata and ads.txt authorisation while the advertising setup is reviewed. Those records do not load advertisements or Google’s advertising consent service. The current Analytics permission is not advertising consent. If advertising is launched later, the certified Google consent message will replace the Analytics-only message and ask for the relevant choices; previous Analytics-only acceptance will not automatically grant advertising permission.

The homepage journey and Postcards reminders save only local dismissal preferences to avoid repeatedly interrupting your visit. These preferences do not check, reveal or change your newsletter subscription or social-media following. External social links contact the relevant platform only when you choose to open them. Read the cookie and player notice.

Videos, Instagram and other external services

Videos hosted with this website do not load a social platform. An external player connects only after you choose to load it. External platforms then process technical information and may use cookies under their own policies. Following an external link also takes you to that provider. See the cookie and player notice.

The contact form stores your name, reply email, chosen topic, message and any optional project details privately in Firebase. A background service using GitHub Actions and Brevo delivers the enquiry to Dainyel’s fixed owner mailbox after production activation; the test website saves messages only in a separate private test inbox and sends no contact mail. The form clearly reports if the service is unavailable. Enquiries are used to reply, not to subscribe you to marketing. Contact records are kept for up to 90 days and reviewed for removal by the owner. The legal basis for replying to your enquiry is your consent. Submitting is optional; the public email link is also available. A saved-message confirmation establishes website receipt, not email delivery. A provider acceptance message confirms receipt by the sending service, not inbox delivery.

Location

Public check-ins show only a city and country chosen by Dainyel. The website does not collect visitors’ device locations. If the owner uses device check-in, rounded coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to find a place name; exact coordinates are not stored in Firestore or displayed publicly.

Your choices and rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your information, withdraw consent, request restriction or portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email Dainyel at support@dainyelonthemove.com. Requests are normally answered within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, you will be told why.

You can also complain to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens ↗, or your local data protection authority. Dainyel does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about visitors using automated processing. Optional personalised advertising by Google, if you permit it, is described above.
