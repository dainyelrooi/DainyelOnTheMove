Your information

Privacy, simply.

What I collect, why I need it and how you can stay in control.

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

Who is responsible?

Dainyel, the Netherlands, is responsible for personal information collected through DainyelOnTheMove. For privacy questions, withdrawal of consent or a request about your information, email support@dainyelonthemove.com. Include only enough information to identify your contribution or sign-up.

Questions and community conversations

If you submit a question, I store the topic, message, time, publication consent and optional nickname. Community questions and replies also store their conversation reference and moderation status. Q&A does not ask for your email address. Only the owner can review private submissions. Approved contributions and my answers are public, so other people and search engines may read them.

The legal basis for receiving and publishing your contribution is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Submitting is optional. You can withdraw publication consent through the contact channel above; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Postcards from Dainyel

The website includes an email sign-up form connected to Brevo. It asks for your email address and agreement to receive weekly destination facts and occasional travel stories, films and updates. Brevo processes your address, consent and subscription records to manage and deliver these emails for Dainyel. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Your address is not published or sold.

After you submit the form with your consent and complete the CAPTCHA, you join the Postcards list immediately. A welcome email follows; no email confirmation click is required. Messages come from postcards@dainyelonthemove.com. You can unsubscribe using the link in the emails or contact support@dainyelonthemove.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

The hosted form uses Google reCAPTCHA to prevent abuse. Brevo and Google may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser details when the form loads and you use it; email delivery and links may also generate technical activity records. Read Brevo’s privacy policy ↗ and Google’s privacy policy ↗ for their processing and international transfer information. The form and its CAPTCHA load near the sign-up section. A direct Brevo form link is also available if the embedded form is unavailable.

Earlier Field Notes interest registrations remain private in Firebase. They have not been imported into Brevo or treated as confirmed subscribers. You can ask for removal through the contact address above.

How long information is kept

  • Unpublished questions and replies: up to 90 days after submission for review. Rejected submissions are removed when no longer needed for moderation.
  • Published contributions: while the contribution remains part of a relevant public conversation, or until you withdraw consent or request removal. These are reviewed at least annually; there is no fixed expiry for the public story archive.
  • Newsletter and earlier Field Notes registrations: up to 24 months after registration, or sooner if you withdraw or the updates service ends. After unsubscribing, only the minimum record needed to honour your opt-out is retained; it is not used to send updates.
  • Privacy requests: only for resolving the request and documenting its outcome, for up to 12 months after closure, unless a specific legal obligation or dispute requires longer retention.

Website records are reviewed and removed manually by the owner; there is no automatic deletion job. Google controls the retention of its service security logs separately. For example, Firebase Hosting retains request IP data for a few months, as described in Firebase’s privacy information ↗.

Website hosting, database and security

Google Firebase provides hosting, Firestore, owner authentication and abuse protection. Network and browser details, including IP addresses and security tokens, may be processed to deliver the site, prevent spam and protect access. I rely on the legitimate interest of operating a secure, reliable website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for necessary security processing. You can object through the contact channel above.

Only the authorised owner can manage private submissions and registrations. Google processes service data under its applicable terms. Some Google services process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States; the relevant safeguards are described in Google’s data processing terms ↗. Optional Google Analytics is described below; advertising features are not enabled.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures visits and page usage to help improve this website. The Google tag stays unloaded until you accept analytics. Rejecting does not prevent reading stories or signing up. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Google processes visit information, page URLs, browser/device details and cookie identifiers under its applicable terms, potentially outside the EEA. Newsletter addresses, question text and account details are not sent as custom analytics events. Configured page locations exclude query strings and arbitrary fragments. Known public panels such as the atlas, Q&A and films use fixed names so their visits can be counted separately. Search text and unknown error-page paths are not sent. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in this integration.

Change or withdraw your choice using Cookie settings in the footer. Your browser choice and Analytics cookies have a configured lifetime of up to six months. Rejecting blocks subsequent measurement and clears this site's Analytics cookies; it does not remove records already sent. Contact support@dainyelonthemove.com about earlier data. The Analytics account is configured to retain event-level data for two months and user-level data for fourteen months. The user-level retention period resets on new activity. These controls do not determine the retention of most aggregated standard reports. Read Google's privacy policy.

Videos, Instagram and other external services

Videos hosted with this website do not load a social platform. An external player connects only after you choose to load it. External platforms then process technical information and may use cookies under their own policies. Following an external link also takes you to that provider. See the cookie and player notice.

The collaboration form prepares a message on your device; it does not send or store that message in the website database. You decide whether to send it through Instagram.

Location

Public check-ins show only a city and country chosen by Dainyel. The website does not collect visitors’ device locations. If the owner uses device check-in, rounded coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to find a place name; exact coordinates are not stored in Firestore or displayed publicly.

Your choices and rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your information, withdraw consent, request restriction or portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email Dainyel at support@dainyelonthemove.com. Requests are normally answered within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, you will be told why.

You can also complain to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens ↗, or your local data protection authority. The site does not use automated decision-making or profiling about visitors.
