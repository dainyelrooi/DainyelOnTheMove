Your choice

Cookies &
privacy choices.

Your choice for optional Analytics. Advertising is off for now. External players load only when you choose them.

Last updated: 26 October 2026. This working notice describes the services currently present in the website.

Before you load a player

Opening the homepage does not load a YouTube or Instagram player. Locally hosted travel videos are served by the website. Optional Google Analytics starts only after you choose Consent in the website’s privacy message. Advertising and the Google advertising consent service are not loaded in the current Analytics-only phase.

When you choose YouTube or Instagram

Pressing a player’s load button connects to that platform. YouTube or Instagram may then receive technical information and use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. The permission applies to that player only and is not saved as a permanent preference. Closing the player removes the embed.

You can instead use the direct platform link. Read Google’s privacy policy ↗ or Instagram’s privacy policy ↗.

Forms and owner sign-in

The website’s Q&A forms use Firebase App Check/reCAPTCHA to help prevent abuse. The private owner area also uses Firebase Authentication to keep the owner signed in. These security services may use browser storage and process technical device and network data. They are separate from optional media players.

Postcards sign-up

The Postcards email form is embedded in the sign-up section and loads only when you first open the form. It connects to Brevo and Google reCAPTCHA for registration and spam protection. When it loads, Brevo and Google may use storage and process device and network information for sign-up, delivery and abuse prevention under their own policies. See the privacy notice for provider links and subscription choices.

Analytics now, advertising later

The website currently offers one privacy message for optional Google Analytics, with Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. Advertising is off while the advertising setup is being reviewed. The Analytics choice works independently of AdSense.

Consent is optional. Without your acceptance, the Analytics tag stays unloaded. Rejecting does not prevent reading the website or using its forms. Analytics stays off on the test website, owner admin, editor previews, error/redirect pages and the dedicated contact, Q&A and sign-up form pages.

Your Analytics-only choice is saved in this browser for up to 180 days. An expired or missing choice asks again. You can review or withdraw it below. Withdrawing stops measurement, clears this site’s Analytics cookies and reloads the page; it does not erase information already sent. The choice is not consent to advertising. If advertising is enabled later, the Google consent message will ask for the applicable choices separately at that time, replacing this Analytics-only message.

When allowed, Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_…) are configured to last up to six months. Read the privacy notice and Google’s cookie information.

Remembering your popup preference

The homepage can show a small journey or Postcards reminder after you have spent time browsing. Closing it or choosing Already following saves a local preference so it does not keep returning. This does not identify you, subscribe you, contact a social platform or record whether you actually follow an account. Clearing browser storage resets this preference.

Privacy and cookie settings

Review or withdraw your optional Analytics choice. Advertising stays off. This does not control external players you deliberately load or necessary security checks.

Your browser controls

You can clear site data or manage cookies through your browser. Blocking security-related storage can affect sign-in or protected forms. See the privacy notice for the information collected by website features.
