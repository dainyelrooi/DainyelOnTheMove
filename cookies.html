Cookies &
video players.

The external player stays unloaded until you choose to open it.

Last updated: 15 October 2026. This working notice describes the services currently present in the website.

Before you load a player

Opening the homepage does not load a YouTube or Instagram player. Locally hosted travel videos are served by the website. Optional Google Analytics loads only after you accept analytics; advertising features are not enabled.

When you choose YouTube or Instagram

Pressing a player’s load button connects to that platform. YouTube or Instagram may then receive technical information and use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. The permission applies to that player only and is not saved as a permanent preference. Closing the player removes the embed.

You can instead use the direct platform link. Read Google’s privacy policy ↗ or Instagram’s privacy policy ↗.

Forms and owner sign-in

The website’s Q&A forms use Firebase App Check/reCAPTCHA to help prevent abuse. The private owner area also uses Firebase Authentication to keep the owner signed in. These security services may use browser storage and process technical device and network data. They are separate from optional media players.

Postcards sign-up

The Postcards email form is embedded in the sign-up section and loads near that section. It connects to Brevo and Google reCAPTCHA for registration and spam protection. When it loads, Brevo and Google may use storage and process device and network information for sign-up, delivery and abuse prevention under their own policies. See the privacy notice for provider links and subscription choices.

Optional Google Analytics

Choose Accept analytics or Reject analytics in the notice. No Google Analytics script loads before acceptance. Your choice is saved locally for up to six months. Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_…) are configured to last six months. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice; rejecting stops subsequent measurement and clears this site’s Analytics cookies. Analytics is disabled on the test website and private admin area. The analytics choice does not control necessary security checks in forms you use. See the privacy notice for processing details.

Your browser controls

You can clear site data or manage cookies through your browser. Blocking security-related storage can affect sign-in or protected forms. See the privacy notice for the information collected by website features.
