Privacy and cookie settings

The website offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options for optional Google Analytics. Accepting and refusing are equally available in the first message. Refusing does not prevent reading the website or using its forms. You can withdraw consent here at any time without signing in.

Withdrawal stops subsequent Analytics measurement, clears this site's Analytics cookies and reloads the page. Your refusal is remembered for up to 180 days. It does not delete earlier records held by Google. For a request about those records, email support@dainyelonthemove.com. These controls do not close external players you have deliberately loaded; close the player to stop its connection.

Analytics and advertising

Google receives visit, page, browser/device and cookie-identifier information after your Analytics consent. Google may process it outside the EEA. Advertising signals and Google signals are disabled. Search text, newsletter addresses, question text, arbitrary URL queries and private account content are not configured Analytics events. The privacy notice explains processing and the last checked account retention settings.

The consent message is for Analytics only. No AdSense advertising tag or Google advertising consent service is loaded in this phase. The public ownership metadata and ads.txt file do not load ads. A future advertising launch requires its own applicable consent choices; your current Analytics choice does not grant advertising permission.

Analytics stays off on the test website, owner admin, editor previews, error/redirect pages and dedicated contact, Q&A and sign-up form pages. Choosing Consent on a test page does not load Analytics there.

When you choose YouTube or Instagram

Locally hosted films do not connect to a social platform. External players remain unloaded until you press their load button. Loading connects to YouTube or Instagram, which receives technical information and may use cookies or similar technologies. The permission applies to that player only and is not saved as a permanent preference. Close the player to remove the embed; this does not erase information already sent or cookies the platform has already set. You can instead use the direct platform link.

Read Google’s privacy policy, Google’s cookie information and Instagram’s privacy policy.

Postcards sign-up and security

The embedded form loads only when you first open it. Brevo and Google reCAPTCHA then process the information needed for registration and abuse protection. Read Brevo’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy. Signing up is optional and never follows automatically from accepting Analytics.

Your browser controls

Browser settings let you clear site data and manage third-party cookies. Blocking necessary security storage can affect protected forms or owner sign-in. Clearing this site's storage also clears your remembered Analytics choice, so the site will ask again with Analytics off until you accept. Read the privacy notice for other data processing and your rights.
