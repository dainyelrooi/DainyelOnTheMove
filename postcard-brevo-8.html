The journey starts today — and I can finally share the first destination: Curaçao.

This first island chapter runs from 28 October to 5 November. Beyond that? I’m keeping the route under wraps for now.

To open this chapter, here are three little things that make Curaçao more than a beautiful patch of blue on the map.

A LITTLE CURIOSITY FOR THE ROAD

01 / DID YOU KNOW?

Colour with a history.

Willemstad’s historic centre and harbour have been on UNESCO’s World Heritage List since 1997. Its architecture brings together European influences and a distinctly Caribbean setting.

02 / DID YOU KNOW?

One word, plenty of meanings.

In Papiamentu, dushi can mean sweet, enjoyable or sweetheart. It can describe food, a person or a lovely moment — a small word with plenty of feeling.

03 / DID YOU KNOW?

There’s a summit beyond the shore.

Curaçao’s highest point is Christoffelberg, at 372 metres. The island has more to its landscape than beaches alone.
