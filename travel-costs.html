Real travel costs.

The plan, the actual spending, and what the records cover.

Planning range: €8,000–€10,000. I would prefer to stay below budget. This is a plan, not money already spent.

No actual expense records have been published yet. There is no spending total or daily average to report.

How the numbers work

Each record stores the amount and my share in integer currency units. A group payment is shown separately from my own cost. Currencies stay separate; no estimated exchange rates are used.

Daily average = my recorded share ÷ inclusive calendar days from the first to last record in the selected currency and period. Missing days and incomplete records mean this is a partial overview, never a complete trip total.
